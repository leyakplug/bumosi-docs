KURA is the governance, staking and acquisition token of the BUMOSI platform.

1,000,000,000

A fixed supply

The cap is reached. Minting is permanently closed.

0.5%

Burned per transfer

Every transfer destroys a share of the token.

Every transfer is checked

Identity and country are verified at each move.

Written safeguards

Any change to the burn rate goes through a 30-day timelock. Any change to the identity registry goes through a 7-day timelock.

Solvent staking

Rewards pay from a pre-funded reserve. Never from other stakers' principal.
